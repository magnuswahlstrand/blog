Serves 4. By Tommy Myllymäki.

## Ingredients

- 4 steaks, good quality (150–200 g each)
- 4 slices of bread, e.g. sourdough
- 1 head of butter lettuce
- 4 egg yolks
- 10 cm fresh horseradish
- Butter and oil, for frying

### Sauce

- 2 dl mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp Swedish (skånsk) mustard
- 2 tsp Dijon mustard

## How to

1. Mix the sauce.
2. Fry the steaks in butter and oil, let rest, then slice.
3. Toast the bread.
4. Spread sauce on the bread and top with lettuce, then the steak.
5. Grate horseradish on top in a pile, like a volcano.
6. Put an egg yolk in the crater.
