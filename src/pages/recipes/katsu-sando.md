## Ingredients

### Tonkatsu sauce

- 100 ml ketchup
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp Japanese soy sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 garlic clove, finely grated

### Cabbage salad

- 150 g cabbage
- ½ tbsp Japanese soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp rice vinegar or white wine vinegar

## How to

1. Mix ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, 2 tbsp Japanese soy sauce, and 2 tbsp honey for the tonkatsu sauce.
2. Add the finely grated garlic clove to the sauce.
3. Prepare the cabbage salad with 150 g cabbage.
4. Season the cabbage with ½ tbsp Japanese soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, and 1 tsp rice vinegar or white wine vinegar.
