## Ingredients

- 6 eggs
- 4 tsp whole milk
- 4 tsp heavy cream
- 10 g butter

## How to

1. Set up sous vide to 75°C.
2. Brown the butter in a pan until dark brown and nutty-smelling. Set aside.
3. Wait until eggs, milk, and cream are room temperature.
4. Whisk eggs, milk, and cream well, then mix in the brown butter.
5. Vacuum seal and cook for 16 minutes. Take out 2–3 times to mix.
6. Eat.
