Serves 4.

- Prep time: 15 minutes
- Total time: 90 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 eggplants
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1/2 lemon
- 1 tsp salt
- 0.6 dl tahini
- 2 tbsp olive oil

## How to

#### Eggplants — 5 min (80 min)

1. Heat the oven to 250° C
2. Poke a few holes in the eggplants (to avoid an explosion in the oven)
3. Cook the eggplant for 25 minutes
4. Flip them
5. Cook for another 25 minutes
6. Take out and cool (> 30 minutes)

#### Garlic and lemon — 3 min (10 min)

1. Crush and mince the garlic and put in a large bowl
2. Add 1 teaspoon of salt and juice of 1/2 lemon
3. Let it rest for 10 minutes

#### Putting it all together — 5 min

1. Cut up the eggplants and scoop out the flesh
2. Cut it finely into a fine paste
3. Put the eggplant into the bowl with the lemon and garlic
4. Add 0.6 dl tahini and 2 tablespoons of olive oil
5. Let sit for 30 min (only gets better the longer it rests)
